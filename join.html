Describe your current technical context.

The application records your current experience, research interests, availability, and preferred contribution areas. It is used to plan onboarding and project roles—not to filter for students who already know every tool.

01

What happens after applying?

Monitor Discord and official club announcements for onboarding, current meeting logistics, laboratory requirements, and project assignments.

02

Can beginners apply?

Yes. Beginners are evaluated on curiosity, care, consistency, and willingness to document and review their work. Technical depth is developed through the program.

03

What should I bring?

A laptop is useful for laboratory work. More important are consistent attendance, careful note-taking, respect for scope and privacy, and the patience to verify results.
